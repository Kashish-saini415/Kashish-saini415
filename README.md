### 🔬 What I'm Working On: Synthetic Data Generation

Benchmarking deep generative models in **PyTorch** on Kaggle T4 GPUs:

- **VAE family:** Vanilla VAE, CVAE, Beta-VAE and VQ-VAE, tuned with **Optuna**
- **GAN:** DCGAN on MNIST and CIFAR10, stabilised with label smoothing and gradient clipping
- **Diffusion:** DDPM with U-Net, EMA and DDIM sampling
- **Evaluation:** FID, LPIPS and Inception Score, plus downstream YOLOv8 classification accuracy
- **Key finding:** diffusion gave better image quality, with mean **FID 88.2 (DDPM) vs 137.6 (DCGAN)**
- **Data scarcity:** training on only 60 images/class raised VAE FID from **211.3 to 320.4 (+51.6%)**
- **Pipeline:** pseudo-labelling generated images and comparing LPIPS-based vs random selection for augmentation
